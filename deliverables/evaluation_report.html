Real embeddings move Config D macro-F1 from 0.929 (mock) to 0.939. The stand-in is a small 384-dim model (no query/passage prefixes); its flatter cosine distribution pushes more traffic to the arbiter under the current gate thresholds. Because embeddings are stored as JSON, the dimensionality change (384 vs 1024) needs only a centroid re-seed, not a schema change. bge-m3 (1024-dim, the exact gateway model) should improve quality further while restoring sharp confidence.
